# Research

Build the claim from evidence, keep the limits, and make the work inspectable.

## Start from the question and decision

State the question, intended audience/decision, competing explanations, required evidence level, time/cost limits, and what an answer would change. Choose Investigate, Experiment, or Write. Use the lightest valid route; do not launch an autonomous experiment without an executable measurement and a justified design.

## Investigate

Decompose the question into claims. Search primary research and authoritative source material; retain exact locators, dates/versions, methods, populations, setting, outcomes, applicability, and limitations. Read sources before citing them. Separate supplied claims, source observations, your inference, contradictions, and unknowns. A secondary summary or model output does not become a primary source by repetition. Show how conflicting evidence changes the conclusion; do not count sources as votes.

Use [investigation and writing roles](references/research-methods.md) to select the retained deeper agent/context guides. Do not invent citations or interpret an inaccessible paper as read. Write the answer the evidence permits, including a useful bounded negative result.

## Experiment

Before outcomes, declare hypothesis, treatment/control, unit of analysis, sample size or minimum detectable effect, measures/guardrails, denominator, error/invalid-trial handling, seeds/repeats, budget, stop rule, and analysis. Freeze the measurement apparatus separately from the intervention. Use [autonomous experiment discipline](references/conducting-autonomous-experiments/METHOD.md) only when its prerequisites hold. Its hash-lock, append-only ledger, error retention, held-out promotion, and judge-calibration warnings are substantive controls, not a substitute for a valid study.

Audit source/data provenance and manifest completeness before trusting a number. Keep failed and ungraded trials visible. Resume only the declared missing/invalid subset, preserving clean records and exact input identities. Distinguish correlated repeats, model variants, and participants. Treat automated judgments as fallible: blind labels where possible, verify literal evidence and semantics, retain disagreements, and state the review independence actually achieved.

For hill-climbing, freeze baseline, intervention paths, fixtures, metric, guardrails, and stopping/cost rule; keep/revert with an append-only ledger. Use held-out confirmation before an effectiveness claim. If apparatus is broken, fix it under a new protocol and preserve the earlier run; do not rewrite outcomes. A cross-vendor judge is not independent proof of truth.

## Write

Build a claim-to-evidence outline before prose. Keep methods sufficient to reproduce; distinguish preregistered and exploratory analysis; show negative/disputed results and limitations. A figure must reflect retained data, with units, uncertainty, and denominator. Use original conference templates and compilation utilities when available, but verify venue requirements from current official sources. Generated illustrations are not data figures. No invented authors, affiliations, citations, or results.

## Output

Return the bounded answer/manuscript/study, claim-source map, methods and raw-record locators, actual checks, uncertainty, unresolved evidence, and the smallest next step. Optional engine checks establish software behavior only; a packaged skill, power calculation, judge score, or self-test does not establish research novelty or user benefit.
